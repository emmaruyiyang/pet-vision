Pet Vision
Motion visualisations

Two pages that play back in the browser. Everything they draw comes from files that already exist — aoi/, viewer/data/ and imu/. No new model, nothing re-segmented.
01

Gaze by scene Heat map + scanpath →

All 19 trials, switched by scene. Pick a scene and its three conditions — human eye height, dog eye height, dog height through a dichromat transform — sit side by side. The View control swaps every plate between the aggregate heat map, which shows where the time went, and the live scanpath, which keeps the order it went in. Under each: the dwell stripe, and each class's dwell share with how far it sits from that class's share of the image.

From viewer/data/*.gaze_px.csv + eyetracking_n1/P01_trials.csv + aoi/gaze_aoi_shares.csv · n = 1 (P01)
 02

Human / dog live AOI Walk →

Five minutes of the walk, 05:00 to 10:00, with both viewpoints playing side by side and every readout advancing with the picture: composition shares, openness, obstacle cover, divergence between the two views, IMU motion and turn rate. The timeline is clickable.

From aoi/aoi_shares.csv · aoi/depth_metrics.csv · aoi/divergence_tv.csv · imu/imu.csv · imu/stops.csv
These two pages load their data with fetch, which the file:// origin blocks, so from a folder they need python3 docs/serve.py and then localhost:8777 — and that server, not python3 -m http.server, because the latter ignores byte ranges and the video will not seek.

To hand either one to someone without any of that, build the double-clickable copies: python3 scripts/bundle_viz_html.py writes them to docs/standalone-build/.
