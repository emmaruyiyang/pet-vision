Other indicators

Openness and obstacle cover come from monocular depth; divergence is the total-variation distance between the two compositions; motion comes from the IMU.
Why this five minutes — picked out of the full 35 minutes for high divergence together with few seconds the segmentation judged too dark or occluded: 8 of these 300 seconds (2.7%), and all of it outdoors. The sedentary indoor block, 19.0–28.6 min, is not in here.
Two traps — ground excludes grass, which ADE20K files under vegetation; and distances ending _cam are in camera heights, not metres. Far-field depth is not trustworthy in this data, near field is.
